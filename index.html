ABOUT TCOSC

An open community for Tanzania's computing ecosystem.

Tanzania Computing Open Source Community (TCOSC) is a national open computing community built around knowledge, practical skills, open-source contribution, research, collaboration, and mentorship.

TCOSC provides a shared environment where people can learn technical skills, put those skills into practice, build real projects, exchange knowledge, contribute to open source, conduct research, and help others grow.

The community is designed to connect learners, developers, researchers, educators, institutions, contributors, and mentors through practical technical work.

Learn more about TCOSC →

Our principle

Learn. Build. Share. Contribute.

Knowledge becomes more valuable when people use it, improve it, document it, and make it available for others to build upon.
