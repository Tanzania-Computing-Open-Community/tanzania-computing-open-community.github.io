About TCOSC

A community building Tanzania's computing future through open source.

Tanzania Computing Open Source Community connects people, knowledge, projects and opportunities around computing through open-source practice, collaboration, research and continuous learning.

Who We Are

A shared space for Tanzania's computing community.

TCOSC is a community built around the idea that computing knowledge becomes more valuable when people can learn, build and improve together.

The community brings together people with different levels of experience and different technical interests. Students, developers, researchers, educators and computing practitioners can participate through practical work, knowledge sharing, open-source projects and collaboration.

Open source is central to this approach because it makes technical work visible, shareable and improvable. A useful idea can become a project. A project can become a learning resource. A contribution can help someone else learn, build or solve a problem.

Our Focus

People, knowledge and practical computing work.

TCOSC is designed around participation rather than passive membership. The community grows through people learning, creating, sharing, reviewing and helping others.

Why TCOSC Exists

Computing grows stronger when knowledge does not remain isolated.

TCOSC exists to create a practical environment where computing knowledge and technical work can move between people, projects and institutions.

Knowledge

Connect useful knowledge.

Learning materials, technical explanations and practical experience can be shared instead of remaining isolated with individual learners or groups.

Practice

Turn learning into practice.

Computing skills develop through experimentation, implementation, troubleshooting and working with real technical problems.

Collaboration

Make collaboration easier.

Open-source workflows provide a practical way for people with different skills and experience to work together.

Continuity

Preserve and improve technical work.

Documentation, repositories and maintained projects allow useful work to remain accessible and continue developing beyond its original creator.

Direction

What TCOSC is working toward.

Vision

A strong, connected and continuously learning computing community in Tanzania.

A community where people can discover knowledge, develop practical skills, build meaningful technology, conduct research and contribute to work that others can learn from and improve.

Mission

Connect people, knowledge, projects and opportunities through open-source computing.

TCOSC supports this mission by creating pathways for learning, practice, building, research, knowledge sharing, collaboration, contribution and mentorship.

Community Responsibility

Building trust through responsible participation.

TCOSC is intended to grow through transparent, responsible and constructive participation.

Community resources and repositories should be treated as shared technical spaces. Contributors are encouraged to document their work, respect contribution guidelines, review changes carefully and help maintain the quality of resources over time.

As the community develops, its governance and working practices can evolve with the needs of its participants. The objective is to build useful structures without creating unnecessary barriers to participation.

Responsibility

Build openly. Contribute responsibly. Improve together.

Open source works best when openness is matched by accountability, documentation, review and respect for other contributors.
