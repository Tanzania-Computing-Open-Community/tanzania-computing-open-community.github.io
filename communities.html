TCOSC Communities

Find your place in Tanzania's computing community.

TCOSC brings people together around practical areas of computing where they can learn, build, share knowledge, collaborate and contribute through open source.

Core Communities

Three connected technical communities.

Each community has its own technical focus while remaining part of the wider TCOSC ecosystem.

01
Computing Students

Learn with other computing students.

A community space for computing students across Tanzanian universities and colleges to share technical knowledge, develop practical skills and collaborate on computing work.

  • Learning and technical skills
  • Practical computing work
  • Student projects
  • Peer knowledge sharing
  • Final-year project experience
  • Collaboration
Explore Computing Students →
02
Cybersecurity

Learn and practice cybersecurity responsibly.

A technical community for people interested in cybersecurity learning, defensive security, security research and responsible technical exploration.

  • Cybersecurity learning
  • Security labs and practice
  • CTFs and challenges
  • Defensive security
  • Vulnerability research
  • Technical writeups
Explore Cybersecurity →
03
Software Development

Build and improve software together.

A community for building, sharing and improving software through open-source development and collaborative engineering.

  • Software engineering
  • Application development
  • Web and mobile development
  • APIs and backend systems
  • Developer tooling
  • Open-source projects

One Ecosystem

The communities are connected, not isolated.

Technical problems rarely fit inside one discipline. TCOSC communities can work together when projects require different skills and perspectives.

Students × Development

Learn through real software work.

Computing students can gain practical development experience by participating in open-source software work and collaborating with experienced contributors.

Students × Cybersecurity

Develop security skills through practice.

Students interested in cybersecurity can learn through responsible security exercises, research and technical collaboration.

Development × Cybersecurity

Build with security in mind.

Software development and cybersecurity contributors can collaborate on secure applications, security tooling and technical research.

Across TCOSC

Different skills can become one project.

A single open-source project may bring together students, developers, security practitioners, researchers and other contributors.

Participation

There is more than one way to participate.

TCOSC communities are open to people at different stages of their computing journey and with different technical interests.

Computing Students

Learn, practice, build projects and connect with other people developing computing skills.

Developers

Build software, review code, maintain projects and share engineering experience.

Cybersecurity Practitioners

Participate in security learning, responsible research, defensive practice and technical work.

Researchers

Explore technical questions, collaborate on research and share findings.

Educators

Share knowledge, practical experience and educational resources with the community.

Open-Source Contributors

Contribute code, documentation, testing, review and other useful work.

Find Your Starting Point

Choose a community and start exploring.

You do not need to be an expert to participate. Start with the area closest to your interests, explore the available work and find a useful way to learn or contribute.
